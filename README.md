# Lynqo

Your Mac becomes a private portal for your phone. Send photos, videos, files and copied text between your phone and your computer over your own Wi‑Fi: no app on the phone, no cable, no account, no cloud.

**[Download Lynqo for Mac](https://github.com/maheshchambhare/lynqo-release/releases/latest/download/Lynqo.dmg)** · [Website](https://lynqo.mcrudra.com) · [All releases](https://github.com/maheshchambhare/lynqo-release/releases)

## Install

1. Download `Lynqo.dmg` and open it.
2. Drag Lynqo into Applications, then open it from there.
3. Scan the code Lynqo shows with your phone's camera. Your phone opens Lynqo in its browser, and your Mac asks before it connects.

Lynqo needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 12 or later. Phones and other computers need only a modern browser on the same Wi‑Fi.

## Updates

Lynqo checks this page for new versions when it opens and every few hours, and asks before installing. You can check yourself in Settings › General › Updates, or turn the checks off there. Nothing about your Mac is sent when it checks.

## Free and Pro

Lynqo Free connects one device, with files both ways at full speed. Lynqo Pro adds unlimited devices, device to device sending, Throw to any screen and the shared clipboard, with a 7-day free trial. See [lynqo.mcrudra.com](https://lynqo.mcrudra.com/pricing).

## Privacy

Files and text go straight between your devices on your Wi‑Fi and are never uploaded. The only calls Lynqo makes outside your Wi‑Fi are checking a Pro key with Dodo Payments and checking this page for updates. Details: [lynqo.mcrudra.com/privacy](https://lynqo.mcrudra.com/privacy).
